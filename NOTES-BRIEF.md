# Service × area notes — writing brief

6 services × 15 areas = 90 notes.

Two or three sentences each. The question every note answers:

> **What is true about doing THIS service in THIS area that is not true of
> the same service one neighbourhood over, or of a different service on the
> same street?**

Substrate, what actually fails here, access, scaffold, planning. Specifics,
not adjectives. Leave a slot empty rather than padding it — the block is
omitted when empty, and an empty slot costs less than a generic one.

Paste into `src/lib/serviceAreaNotes.ts` against the key shown.
