A stone garden wall built to last — proper foundations, natural Bath stone, and landscaping that makes it look like it was always part of the garden.

Site cleared and levels set. Foundations dug deep enough to stop any movement. Stone laid in courses that hold their line, with drainage built in behind so water pressure never pushes the wall forward. Pointing finished to match the surroundings — the kind that weathers in, not washes out.

Then the landscaping tied back in. Levels, planting, and hard standing so the wall sits naturally in the garden rather than looking like it was bolted on yesterday.

## Highlights

- Proper foundations — dug deep, poured solid
- Natural Bath stone laid in courses
- Drainage built in behind the wall
- Pointing that weathers in, not washes out
- Landscaping tied back in — levels, planting, hard standing
- A wall that looks like it was always there
